# Decisions

- **1 Nov 2026 (P52 presentation).** Following the user-approved design research, loaded results use a compact media-first workspace with separate evidence, editable-draft and saved-round comparison views. Dark/light appearance is session-local. The time axis supplements the full claim list and preserves recorded timing; changed caption highlighting never rewrites wording. No API, model, evaluation or review-eligibility behavior changes. See [the design research](./p52-design-research.md) and [verification](../reviews/p52-user-verification-2026-11-01.md).

- **1 Nov 2026 (P52 implementation).** The local React page consumes P51 and receives progress over SSE. Screening types are generated from the schema; private media and exact evidence crops are served through confined API reads. Demo outcomes are revealed from committed ruling metadata, while scoreboard rates require an explicit registered test-run selection in `results/final.md`. Missing final assets/metrics stay unavailable rather than being replaced with development or replay results. See [the web guide](../web.md).

One dated line per decision the founders take. Changing a product rule needs all three founders and a line here.

- **1 Nov 2026 (P27).** The judge gives each claim one of three verdicts: possible breach, on hold or no issue. The issue's "needs a person" verdict is dropped, since the schema has no such flag kind and every flag is for a person to decide (rule 4). A rule about the ad as a whole (2.1, 2.3) is cited on the claim that carries the breach, with no schema change.
- **1 Nov 2026 (P31).** The claim recall the team quotes is the share of breaching claims caught among those the reconstruction carried (`claim_recall_carried`), since a reconstruction can drop a claim-bearing name and the screen cannot catch what the ad no longer says. The figure over every ground-truth claim stays registered beside it in every run. Taken by NEESAY; the other two to confirm.
- **1 Nov 2026 (P41).** Piper moved from the `fixtures` dependency group to the optional extra `render` (`uv sync --extra render`), because the live fix path renders drafts with it. This supersedes the P05 placement.
- **1 Nov 2026 (P27).** Judge answers are validated when parsed, so a cached or replayed answer meets the same contract as a live one, and a `no_issue` verdict that cites sources or suggests wording is an error. A claim that names a substance keeps only the register and on-hold citations, and the suggested wording, shortlisted for that substance; one naming none may cite any entry shortlisted for the ad. A citation or suggestion that fails this is dropped and the flag kept, and a flag left citing nothing fails the check (rule 3). Registered dev results predate this and need a fresh dev run before they describe it. Taken by ADI-0519 and NEESAY; Neesay to confirm.
- **1 Nov 2026 (P28).** A screening without a finished check is incomplete, even when no stage failed; when extraction finished and found no claims, the check finishes without a model call. A report shows only the crop of the cited frame and refuses crop paths outside the crop folder. Taken by ADI-0519; the other two to confirm.
- **1 Nov 2026 (P31).** A run is not registered if any screening lacks a finished check; its per-item rows are still written. Taken by ADI-0519; the other two to confirm.
- **1 Nov 2026 (P51).** The API is a local, single-process service. Uploads are raw request bodies under opaque IDs, and everything derived from one (ingest output, model cache, results, audit) lives in that upload's folder and expires with it, after 24 hours by default; a retention journal records storage and deletion with IDs only. Jobs record metadata-only progress; a restart marks unfinished jobs failed rather than repeating model calls, and a job still running when its input expires is failed so the input can be deleted. Approval reuses P41's audit. Authentication and public deployment are separate work (`docs/api.md`). Taken by ADI-0519; the other two to confirm.

On split v1 dev (`check-20261102T002108`) it catches 185 of 250 claims with a matching rule, against 190 to 194 for v5, within run-to-run noise, and 152 with every rule right, against 55; rule precision is 475 of 577 and rule recall 428 of 506. - **2 Nov 2026 (P71).** Rulings snapshot `2026-11-02` is the default. It comes from 15 searches over formal rulings from 1 Jan 2015, and holds 114 in-scope rulings against 112 in `2026-11-01`, so a wider search adds almost nothing: the precedent pool is limited by evaluation hygiene, not by the search. - **2 Nov 2026 (P71).** Evaluation runs give each item its own precedent index: test rulings and every ruling on a test advertiser stay out, and dev rulings are in except the item's own and any on its advertiser, and an item on a ruling sees only rulings published before its own. The date rule matters: without it, 302 of 499 citations on `screen-20261102T141820` were to rulings out on or after the item's own ruling, 169 the same day as siblings from one ASA sweep, so that run is void and is not quoted. This replaces the 1 Nov (P27) rule that kept every split ruling out; the per-item index on split v2 holds 32 to 57 rulings, against 34 before. The CLI, API and agent tools keep every split ruling out, so a reconstruction in the demo never cites its own ruling (`docs/product-rules.md`, rule 5). Taken by NEESAY; the other two to confirm.
- **2 Nov 2026 (P71).** Precedent retrieval stays BM25. In a blind read of the top pick for 40 dev claims, BM25, MiniLM embeddings and rule-filtered embeddings scored 41, 42 and 40 of 80, with 10 to 14 of 40 picks unrelated; Spark choosing from the pool was no better and added 9 to 18 s per ad. About a third of claims have no similar ruling in the pool, so the remaining fix is for the judge to cite a ruling only when it concerns a similar claim. That is judge wording, for Neesay or ADI-0519. Taken by NEESAY; the other two to confirm.
- **2 Nov 2026 (P71).** Every run registers `claim_recall_carried_by_chance` and `claim_recall_carried_weighted` next to `claim_recall_carried`. The judge cites a median of four CAP rules per flag, and `claim_recall_carried` credits a catch if any one of them fits. On `screen-20261102T014816`, the same flags citing as many rules at random would catch 91 of 238 claims against 178, and crediting each catch by its share of right rules gives 115. The P33 bar should be set with all three in view. Taken by NEESAY; the other two to confirm.
- **2 Nov 2026 (P70).** A flagged line counts as matching a ground-truth claim, for `unmatched_lines_per_positive_ad`, when it quotes the claim at any length or is mostly a piece of it, under a related rule; claim recall keeps the tight match. Of the 149 unmatched lines on `screen-20261102T014816`, 48 carried a claim the ASA cited, 37 of them as fragments (`docs/notes/noise-lines.md`). Rescored, judge_v5 has 114 unmatched lines on 25 positive ads (was 149) and judge_v6 116 (was 152), so 116/25 is the judge_v6 baseline for P70. Taken by NEESAY; the other two to confirm.
