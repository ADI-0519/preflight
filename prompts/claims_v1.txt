List every claim in this ad that the UK CAP Code could apply to.
A claim is any statement, word, name or picture that tells the viewer something about the product, what it does or how it compares.
Include health claims: anything saying or suggesting that the product, a substance in it or the food affects the body, the mind, mood, sleep, energy, weight, appearance, or a disease or its symptoms.
Include claims to prevent, treat or cure a disease or condition, and any naming of a condition the product is said to help with.
Include nutrition claims: anything about the amount of a nutrient, energy or ingredient.
Include general claims about health or well-being, such as feeling better, more balanced or more alive.
Include claims about the product that could mislead: its ingredients, purity, strength, results, how fast it works, proof, testing, popularity or price.
Include comparisons with other products, earlier formulas, medicines or treatments.
Include endorsements and testimonials by doctors, health professionals, celebrities or customers, and references to studies or experts.
Include implied claims: a picture, a before-and-after image, a figure or a mix of words and pictures that suggests an effect without stating it.
A single word or name is a claim when it suggests an effect, such as a product name that sounds like a medicine or a term that suggests a health benefit.
Ignore website navigation, cookie banners, footers and other page furniture that is not part of the ad.

For an explicit claim, quote the smallest passage of the ad that makes the claim, exactly as spoken or shown, keeping its spelling.
Give a sentence that makes several claims as several entries, one per claim.
Give a claim made more than once as one entry that cites every place it appears.
For an implied claim, quote the words it rests on, or describe in one short sentence what the picture suggests when no words carry it.
Give the substance or nutrient the claim is about as the ad names it, or null when it names none.
Cite as evidence every transcript segment, on-screen text and visual track the claim comes from, using only the IDs listed below.
Watch the ad itself as well as reading the lists: the lists may miss text, and pictures carry implied claims.
