## Mission and non-negotiable rules

PreFlight screens ads for possible issues; it does not issue a Meta platform decision or legal advice. Follow [product-rules.md](./product-rules.md) and [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md). In particular:

- Every user-visible flag must be linked to evidence and source provenance.
- Suggested wording must come verbatim from an authorised register entry; a model must not invent it.
- A human makes the final decision. Do not label an ad approved, compliant, safe, or guaranteed to pass.
- Protect held-back evaluation data and customer/partner material. Never commit secrets or raw customer media; use synthetic fixtures.
- Do not call external services, use credentials, commit, push, or deploy without explicit user approval.
