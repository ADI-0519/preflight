# Hackathon research worker prompt

Research one question for the PreFlight hackathon strategy. Do not edit product code.

## Required method

1. Prefer official Meta/Cerebral Valley rules, model documentation, official winner announcements, and original research papers.
2. Use secondary sources only to locate primary sources or identify a claim that needs confirmation.
3. Record the publication date and distinguish confirmed facts from inference.
4. Check for conflicts between sources and preserve the conflict.
5. Do not infer that an absent public page proves that a rule does not exist.

## Output

```text
Question:
Confirmed facts:
Primary sources:
Secondary sources:
Inference for PreFlight:
Contradictions or gaps:
Recommended action:
```

The recommended action must be one of: proceed, verify with organiser, defer, or reject. Do not recommend feature work where a legal or eligibility gate is unresolved.
